## 📱 How Users Install Your App

### On Android (Chrome/Edge):
1. Visit your website
2. See "Install Pulse Chat" banner at bottom
3. Tap "Install App"
4. App icon appears on home screen
5. Opens like WhatsApp!

### On iPhone (Safari):
1. Visit your website
2. Tap Share button (square with arrow)
3. Scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add"
5. App icon appears on home screen

## 🎨 Next Step: Create App Icons

You need to create app icons in different sizes. Here's how:

### Option 1: Use the Icon Generator (Easiest)
1. Open `generate-icons.html` in your browser
2. Upload your logo (use `/public/me.webp` or `/public/ajibola.jpg`)
3. Click "Generate Icons"
4. Click "Download All"
5. Save all icons to `/public/` folder with names:
   - `icon-72.png`
   - `icon-96.png`
   - `icon-128.png`
   - `icon-144.png`
   - `icon-152.png`
   - `icon-192.png`
   - `icon-384.png`
   - `icon-512.png`

### Option 2: Use Online Tool
1. Go to https://realfavicongenerator.net/
2. Upload your logo
3. Download the generated icons
4. Place in `/public/` folder
